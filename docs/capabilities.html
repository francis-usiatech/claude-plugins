Skills

SkillCapability
clarifyOne question at a time until purpose, success, constraints and scope edges are clear; ends with an understanding note. Writes no files.
to-specWrites the understanding to docs/specs/ with requirements R1…, acceptance criteria AC1… and open questions.
to-ticketsSplits an approved spec into small tickets with blockers, filed in GitHub or Jira.
approve-planRoutes small or large, records the plan after a governance pre-flight, handles scope changes, archives the plan.
tddRed → green → refactor with evidence for each cycle.
debugReproduce → localize → reduce → approved fix → regression guard test.
mutation-testBreaks each fix point on purpose and reports surviving mutations as missing tests.
verifyRuns the gate (steps, conditional steps, fake-pass check, timeouts) and checks each acceptance criterion.
releaseScoped staging, gate, two-stage review, conventional commit, PR, merge, tag, clean-up.
release-stepsThe mechanical release steps in a forked subagent; returns a short summary.
setup-engineeringCreates .claude/engineering.json, checks the AGENTS.md layout, installs the CI kit after approval.
user-guide-updateUpdates affected user-guide pages and the changelog after a user-visible change.
