- AC2 (R8): in `tests/workflow-rules.test.mjs`, two workflows give `wf-untrusted-input`:
  - (i) a step that runs `echo "T=$TITLE" >> "$GITHUB_ENV"` with `TITLE` from the PR title, then a later step with `${{ env.T }}` in `run:`;
  - (ii) step `s` that writes `t=$TITLE` to `$GITHUB_OUTPUT`, then a later step with `${{ steps.s.outputs.t }}` in `run:`.

  A heredoc write (`echo "T<<EOF"`, `echo "$TITLE"`, `echo EOF`, grouped as `{ …; } >> "$GITHUB_ENV"`) also taints. A step that writes only fixed text (`echo "X=1" >> "$GITHUB_ENV"`) and a later `${{ env.X }}` give no finding.
  - Evidence: workflow-rules suite "untrusted input passed through $GITHUB_ENV or a step output (R8)": $GITHUB_ENV, heredoc, $GITHUB_OUTPUT, step-env and no-finding cases red before, green after (222 pass); 6 R8 mutations CAUGHT, including the GITHUB_*/RUNNER_* survivor after Francis's added test (shown, 2026-10-20T01:16Z, re-confirmed by the 01:16:58Z gate run)
- AC3 (ESP-103): in `tests/workflow-rules.test.mjs`, under `issue_comment` and under `pull_request_target`, `actions/checkout` with `ref: ${{ steps.pr.outputs.sha }}` gives `wf-privileged-checkout`. The same step under `pull_request` gives none.
  - Evidence: "should flag a privileged checkout of a ref taken from a step output (ESP-103)" red before, green after; mutation "ESP-103: checkout ref from a step output not PR code" CAUGHT (shown, 2026-10-20T01:16Z, re-confirmed by the 01:16:58Z gate run)
